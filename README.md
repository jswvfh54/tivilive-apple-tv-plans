## How to set up IPTV on Apple TV

The current TiviLive installation guide points Apple TV users toward UHF. The process is basically a five-minute admin task, not a science experiment.

First, open the App Store on your Apple TV and install **UHF — Love Your IPTV**. Then open the app and add a new playlist. Select **Xtream Codes** and enter the username, password, and server URL from your TiviLive welcome email. TiviLive says the channel list and VOD content should then load in the player.

The UHF listing confirms that the app supports Apple TV and Xtream-based sources, with the current Apple TV version requiring tvOS 17 or later.

For connection speed, TiviLive recommends at least **15 Mbps**, with **25 Mbps or more recommended for 4K/UHD**. It also recommends a wired Ethernet connection when possible.

That last part is worth remembering. When video buffers, the immediate instinct is to blame the IPTV service. Sometimes the real culprit is Wi-Fi doing its usual mysterious little dance.
